How the client learns the flag state: only indirectly — the preview endpoints return configured: boolean in their payload. There is no dedicated "feature status" endpoint. This is why PhotoImportPanel can't easily hide its button today: it doesn't fetch a preview until you click.

Kill-switch design rule

Gate at the narrowest waist. GooglePlacesPhotosClient is the only class that talks to Google. A flag check inside its requireConfigured() covers every present and future caller automatically — including retry() and processDownload(). Per-method gating at the service layer (the current design) is exactly how retry() got missed.

Out of scope of this flag

GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY also powers geocoding (maps.controller.ts). That is a separate, much cheaper cost surface and is not covered by ENABLE_GOOGLE_PHOTO_IMPORT.
