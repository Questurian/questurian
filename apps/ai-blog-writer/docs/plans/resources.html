Existing builder: structured days

Snapshot: September 14, 2026. Seven built-in templates and 2 saved custom templates. These are existing builder data, not approved decisions for the new pipeline. Full Day Balanced is the default.

Each template fixes an ordered set of stop slots. Each slot carries a time of day, allowed place categories, preferred categories, search cues, and optional exclusions. Times are dayparts, not clock times or durations.
