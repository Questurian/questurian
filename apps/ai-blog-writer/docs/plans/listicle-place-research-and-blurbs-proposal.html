A restaurant does not have one permanent writing angle. It has evidence about ceviche, cocktails, service, setting, history and other strengths. Each list draws a different selection from that evidence.

TermWhat it meansExample
Discovery angle — already existsA search instruction that returned the place. A research lead, not proof.“Lima bars serving ají-glazed wings”
Research facet — proposedA stable question family used to find and retrieve evidence.Signature offering; technique; value; setting
Topic — proposedWhat a claim concerns; independent of its evidence kind.ceviche, cocktails, chicken wings, restaurant-wide
Claim — exists, needs extensionsOne attributed assertion with source, scope and dates.A reviewer describes the ceviche’s fish cut and citrus balance.
Entry angle — proposedThe supported reason this entry leads the way it does in one article.A cevichería whose cocktail program makes dinner worth lingering over.

Keep existing kind (review, history, award…) but add facets and topics. A review can describe cocktails or ceviche; “review” alone cannot retrieve the right material. “Hidden gem” is not a fact tag to stamp on a venue. It needs a concrete, defensible meaning in the article, or we leave it out.

The trope catalogue becomes a research library

Start with these twelve reusable facets. They are questions to investigate, not twelve required sections or twelve calls per restaurant. Article context activates a subset; incidental useful evidence is retained for future lists.

For wings: activate signature, technique, customer experience and value; add setting/occasion where the discovery evidence suggests it. For a broad restaurant list: widen toward people, history, recognition and the overall meal. For cocktails: center drinks, preparation and bartender, with setting as support.

{place} {district} alitas salsa especialidad / ceviche especialidad / cóctel de autor
Named item, specific description, source; a menu proves availability, not excellence.

Documented technique or ingredient; never infer from cuisine label.

Individual observations with date. Small samples remain small samples.

{place} alitas carta precio porción promoción
Dated price, serving size and conditions. Cheap requires a stated comparison.
Room and settingWhat experience does the place offer?
{place} ambiente terraza barra salón
Branch-specific physical details, supported by source text or reviewed assets.
Occasion and companyWho would this suit, and why?
{place} fútbol pantallas grupos reservas
Observable evidence behind the fit. “Good for groups” is an inference, labeled as such.
Drinks and pairingsIs there a second reason to visit?
{place} cócteles carta bartender cerveza
Named drinks or beer program; only research deeply when relevant to the list.
People and craftWho shapes the experience?
{place} chef fundador bartender entrevista
Named person, role, publication date; current role needs current support.
History and continuityWhat lasting story is real?
{place} historia fundación aniversario
Dated events; historical award/founding facts remain historical.
Independent recognitionWhat was recognized, by whom, when?
{place} premio reseña crítica guía
Exact award/category/year; restaurant award cannot become a wings award.
Useful visit detailWhat would change someone’s decision?
{place} alitas horario cocina reserva agotado
Kitchen availability, constraints, actionable detail; avoid copying general hours into prose.
Limits and tradeoffsWhat should the reader know?
{place} alitas opiniones espera picante porciones
Specific attributed limitation; no consensus claim from one complaint.

Not every facet must fill. A new neighborhood restaurant can succeed through a distinctive preparation and two useful specifics. Missing awards or a founder interview is not a defect. A facet may be unsearched, covered, thin, inaccessible, conflicting or not applicable; these states describe our knowledge, not the venue’s quality.

Order matters: B → D → if D finds a researchable core gap, C → D again. Otherwise proceed to E, or show “needs your input.” C is a conditional branch, not a mandatory stage before D. F warnings remain reviewable; no endless gate after writing.

Bounded work: one active gather per profile/topic request; start with three concurrent places; save each completed call immediately. Cancelling leaves completed profiles reusable. A transport failure is recorded separately from an empty successful answer. Retry a failed paid call only through an explicit retry action.

What stays in the bank

The durable asset is a sourced, topic-aware place profile. Article-specific fit, selected lead and prose belong to the entry. A place rejected for a wings list can remain useful for cocktails.

Topic tags retrieve candidate evidence; D judges its relevance. Reuse is not a permanent “angle approved” badge. The same fact can be sufficient for a supporting sentence but insufficient for the lead of another list.

Cache rules that make this safe

  • History and dated awards remain historical. Never silently turn an old accolade into current standing.
  • Prices/promotions: proposed 30-day recheck; time-bound offers expire on their stated end date. Current menu/personnel: proposed 90-day recheck. These are pilot defaults, not guarantees.
  • Customer observations keep their publication dates. Re-fetching an old review does not make the experience recent.
  • Unknown publication date stays unknown. “Found today” is a retrieval date only.
  • Unsearched or inaccessible is not a negative cache hit. A completed thin search can suppress repeated automatic calls for a proposed 14 days, with manual retry available.
  • New article topic triggers a gap check, not full-profile deletion. A failed refresh preserves previous usable evidence and shows the failure.
  • Conflicting claims stay visible until resolved. No automatic “newest sentence wins.” Branch-local facts never become brand-wide by accident.

What the operator actually sees

  1. Approve places. Save current board and manual prep. Explain that this selects research targets, not an irrevocable publication list.
  2. Review one research recipe. “For wings we’ll look for the offering, preparation, wings-specific opinions and value.” Expand to inspect exact planned calls. No per-place questionnaire.
  3. Research selected places. Per-place progress: reused evidence, researching, ready, thin, failed, needs identity review. Completed work appears immediately.
  4. Review evidence and angles. Each entry shows a proposed lead, its cited facts, alternate supported leads and missing material. Change the lead, supply a URL/note, retry a gap, or remove the entry.
  5. Write ready entries. Default uses proposed leads; no requirement to approve 35 dropdowns. Thin entries can be written shorter or held by the operator.
  6. Read and edit drafts. Source links and checker findings stay available. Drafting does not publish or send LM jobs. A later explicit handoff queues the places retained for the article.

A manual observation can be saved with provenance “operator supplied.” It must not masquerade as a newspaper citation. If a source is inaccessible to the tool, let the operator supply a relevant excerpt or observation and keep that distinction visible.

Call budget you can inspect

Count provider invocations, not invented dollar precision. One grounded invocation can contain multiple internal searches. Capture actual tokens, grounding usage and receipts; the September 10 discovery price is not a reliable quote for these larger prompts.

Formula: 1 recipe + (N − cached) gather + F follow-up + N brief + F brief reassessment + N write + N check + R repair + R recheck. Assumes all N eventually drafted, one follow-up maximum per place, no network retries, recipe not already cached. Full cache hits still get article-specific brief/write/check.

Initial ceilings to test: recipe 1,500 output tokens; gather 3,500; follow-up 2,000; brief 1,000; writer 700; checker 1,000. These are caps, not expected usage. Send only relevant facts to each entry; retrieve prior blurbs for that venue, never every profile in the database.

Existing code we should reuse—and revise

profiles.py / profile_store.py already define claims, sightings and past blurbs. profile_research.py currently tries the same prompt up to three times until six claims, excludes menu evidence, and can assign the first source URL to an unattributed claim. Replace those behaviors before wiring it into this phase. gate.py weighs sources and claim count but does not establish article-topic sufficiency; keep it out of automatic selection decisions until redesigned around the entry standard.

places.py offers a separate Google details/reviews path. Keep it optional in the first slice. A small returned review sample is not the full review corpus; retrieving it is a separate non-AI call and must be visible in the budget. Existing board ratings do not contain review text.

Migration, model selection and provider/source access need implementation review. No new scraper, new external service or unbounded source ladder is implied by this proposal.

Three decisions to discuss first

  1. How broad is the first profile? Recommendation: research the current topic plus useful incidental facts. A complete restaurant dossier up front spends money on topics we may never use.
  2. What makes an entry ready? Recommendation: evidence for this list’s promise plus enough concrete detail for a useful paragraph. No universal minimum number of claims and no awards requirement.
  3. Who picks the lead? Recommendation: propose it from the evidence, let the operator change it. Approve the whole research recipe once; review individual exceptions.

This is the key design commitment to test: one reusable place profile, many topic-specific entry briefs, independently written blurbs. We can change the number of calls after the five-place pilot without changing that ownership model.
