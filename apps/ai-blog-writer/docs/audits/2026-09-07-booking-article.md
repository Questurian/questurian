Three restaurants, three different rules, and only one of them publishes a release date you can count backwards from. Here is where each stands as of 7 September 2026, checked on the restaurants' own booking pages.

## Maido: two months out, on the first of the month

Maido's reservations page states the rule outright: "Las reservas se habilitan con 2 meses de anticipación, el primer día de cada mes." The calendar opens two months ahead, on the first day of each month, for parties of one to ten.

That is a date you can compute. A dinner on 12 January 2027 becomes bookable on 1 November 2026. A dinner on 3 December 2026 became bookable on 1 October. Count back two calendar months from your dinner, go to the first of that month, and that is your alarm.

Set the alarm for 00:00 Lima time. Maido publishes the day but not the hour, and Peru stays on UTC-5 all year with no clock change, so the safest assumption is that the calendar turns at midnight there. Have the page loaded before then. The reservations team (reservas@maido.pe, +51 1 313-5100 ext. 21) is a second run at it; the site gives their hours as Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm in one place and 9am to 9pm in another, so call before 6pm to be sure of reaching someone.

Service runs Monday to Saturday with seatings listed from 1:00 pm to 9:00 pm, closed Sunday. The twelve-step menu is S/1,295 without pairings, S/1,985 with the Maido pairing and S/2,525 with Tokujou. Booking asks you to accept a cancellation policy backed by a card guarantee, so read the terms on the confirmation screen rather than after.

## Central and Kjolle: four months on paper, eight weeks in practice

Both pages carry the same sentence: online reservations for one to six people up to four months in advance. Both also carry a second line that changes what you should do with the first. As of today, each page says bookings are available only until the end of October. That is under eight weeks out, not four months, which means the four-month figure is a ceiling on the calendar rather than a promise that your date is loadable now.

Neither restaurant publishes a release day or a release hour. So for these two, the alarm is not a single date; it is a daily habit until your month appears. Check both pages once a day, and check the day your month should logically open under the four-month ceiling.

They share an address, Av. Pedro de Osma 301 in Barranco, and they do not share a schedule. Central serves Monday to Saturday, lunch 12:45 to 13:30 and dinner 19:00 to 20:30. Kjolle serves Tuesday to Saturday, lunch 12:45 to 14:00 and dinner 19:00 to 20:30. If your fixed date is a Sunday, neither is open. If it is a Monday, Kjolle is out. Confirm that before you spend a week refreshing. Parties above six cannot book online at either: those go to reservas@centralrestaurante.com.pe or reservas@kjolle.com. Prices at both include VAT and service charge, and both restaurants run lunch as well as dinner, which gives a flexible reader two shots at each open day rather than one.

## One date that overrides all of this

The World's 50 Best Restaurants 2026 ceremony is in Lima on 4 November, the list's first South American edition, with a week of associated events around it. If your dinner falls in that week, treat all three of these rooms as effectively closed to walk-in luck and build your evening around a booking you already hold.

## The cancellation routine

Cancellation hunting works because it is scheduled, not because you are lucky. Three fixed checks a day, in Lima time:

**00:00.** The calendar day turns. This is also when Maido's monthly release lands on the first.

**09:00.** Reservation offices open. Overnight email cancellations get processed into the system now, and this is the window in which a phone call reaches a human at all three.

**22:30.** The last useful sweep. Mesa247, which handles bookings for all three, runs a waitlist alongside the calendar, and its own terms say a waitlist entry for a service is valid until 23:59 the day before. Tables genuinely move the night before.

Join the waitlist for every date and both services you can eat, not only your first choice. Waitlisting is not a confirmed reservation; the system emails you either way, so it costs nothing to be on several.

## Book Mérito now, tonight

Mérito in Barranco was fourth in Latin America's 50 Best Restaurants 2025 and 26th in the World's 50 Best 2025, above Mayta and in the same conversation as the rooms you are chasing. Juan Luis Martínez cooks Venezuelan memory through Peruvian produce, largely à la carte, with a chef's counter worth asking for by name when you book. Mesa247 lists the average spend at S/130, which is a different order of money from S/1,295 before pairings.

It books through mesa247 at merito.mesa247.pe, or by phone on +51 1 277 1628. Mérito publishes no advance-booking rule and no monthly release, which is the point: its calendar is simply open, with no two-month gate to be caught behind. Its own booking channel lists service Monday to Wednesday 12:00 to 15:00 and 19:00 to 22:00, Thursday to Saturday until 22:15, closed Sunday. Other listings give slightly different days, so take the times on the booking page as the ones that govern. Four to six weeks ahead is the sensible lead; inside that, it is still worth trying, which is not true of the other three.

Hold that table first. Then run the routine. If Maido or Central or Kjolle comes through, you cancel Mérito inside its policy and lose nothing. If none of them does, you already have the evening you wanted.
