01 / Assessment

Better wording alone won’t fix this.

Writing prompts need work. Several problems are code and policy conflicts: fixes in one layer are undone by another.

Scope reviewed: intake, research, fact selection, writing, grounding, audit, and repair. This report presents the preceding audit; it does not claim a new end-to-end test or proven improvements to generated prose.

Seed → Grill → Brief → Research → Selection → Writing → Grounding → Audit / Repair → Draft

Keep the foundation

Frozen evidence packets, preserved caveats, scoped repairs, and retained drafts are worth keeping. Targeted simplification is justified; another rebuild is not.

02 / Findings

Where intent and execution disagree

01Score leakage

“Length is advisory” still triggers failure indirectly.

Length was removed from hard constraints, but enforce_measured_check_ceiling() caps scores whenever any measured boolean fails—including length and paragraph length. An isolated offline check confirmed 9 → 6 for either. That score feeds repair and readiness decisions.

Proposed fix: Cap scores only for explicitly hard checks. Keep length and rhythm measurements advisory.

quality.py · line 110

02Prompt conflict

Form-specific writing still receives generic guide instructions.

Profiles correctly allow narrative openings and endings. Compose also injects SEO instructions requiring a 40–60-word direct answer and closing takeaways. One layer reintroduces the behavior another layer removed.

Proposed fix: Let the form own opening, sections, and ending. Remove structural instructions from SEO. Test the fully assembled prompt, not just form rules.

generation.py · line 10

03Authority conflict

Style instructions claim authority over factual instructions.

Canonical hierarchy puts evidence first. The appended anti-AI block says it overrides any conflicting instruction above. It also says accuracy is settled before writing, while earlier instructions carefully preserve uncertainty and dates.

Proposed fix: Style may change expression, never evidence limits, attribution needed for meaning, or the approved form.

anti_ai_tells.py · line 35

04Failure handling

Failed ranking restores overloaded writer input.

Missing selection correctly blocks writing. Ranking failure still creates a selection with every fact kept. Writing checks that selection exists, not that ranking succeeded. The UI reports failure, but continuation can recreate the hundred-fact article. Partial ranking also succeeds after matching only some IDs; remaining facts are appended in dossier order.

Proposed fix: Require an explicit editorial choice after failed or incomplete ranking before writing.

selection_v4.py · line 1154

05Editorial judgment

Audit treats too many forms like purchasing guides.

Every section is judged on what decision it resolves and which option readers should choose. Useful for comparisons; wrong as a universal standard for profiles, news, history, or essays. The fails_if check also expects one offending sentence. “Names no dishes” and “never develops the argument” are omissions or structural failures; neither necessarily has a sentence to quote.

Proposed fix: Evaluate the brief’s promised outcome. Classify findings as unsupported statement, omission, structure, or style, with evidence appropriate to each.

editorial_v3.py · line 270

06Missing context

Audit is told omitted facts were unavailable.

Audit context says material absent from the draft was either never found or deliberately excluded. It misses a third possibility: the writer ignored a useful selected fact. Repair receives those selected facts, but audit lacks enough information to request them reliably.

Proposed fix: Give audit a compact inventory of selected facts. Keep reserve facts excluded.

03 / Avoidable work

Gather less excess. Send fewer competing rules.

Research expands; selection cuts

Research says “Answer it as fully as you can… Then keep going.” Selection then runs deduplication, restatement folding, main ranking, and separate texture ranking. You pay to gather excess, structure it, then remove it.

Change: give each question a purpose and stopping condition. Retain useful incidental details.

research_v4.py · line 826

The audit carries too much history

Audit template: 6,324 characters. Compose template: 2,778 characters. Both counts exclude injected context. Length alone is not waste, but historical failure stories and overlapping rules belong largely in evaluation fixtures.

Style surgery needs evidence of value

Hyphen quotas and sentence-shape bans can buy extra editing without improving meaning. Keep useful guidance; compare real drafts before spending automatic cleanup calls on cosmetic rules.

Documentation invites regression

The feature’s CONTEXT.md still describes deleted fallback paths and obsolete graph stages. Future agents can mistake intentional removals for missing functionality.

04 / Writing instructions

Keep the voice. Simplify the surrounding stack.

The central voice file is good: specific, unsold, attentive, willing to make supported judgments. The surrounding stack repeats prohibitions and disagrees about structure, authority, and quality.

Editorial judgment: this encourages mechanically compliant prose while reducing narrative range. That effect needs draft comparison to prove.

Proposed compose instruction

Proposal only; not applied. Retain the existing output schema and necessary mechanical rules separately.

Write the article promised by the approved brief in the Questurian voice. Use the selected evidence and supplied material. Preserve qualifications that change meaning: dates, scope, uncertainty, exceptions, and who experienced or observed something. Never invent missing support. Follow the approved form and section plan. Each section should advance the article’s purpose through useful explanation, supported judgment, or concrete detail. Choose the strongest material; selected facts are available, not mandatory. Use specific nouns and active verbs. Vary sentence length naturally. Prefer a clear sentence over a clever construction. Make recommendations when the brief calls for them and evidence supports them. Let the form determine the opening and ending. Report unsupported brief requirements in remaining_gaps, outside the article.

Remove conflicting injected instructions at the same time. Adding this paragraph on top would worsen the stack.

05 / Recommended sequence

Fix mechanisms, then compare drafts.

  1. Close the four execution gaps

    Fix score leakage, prompt authority, form conflicts, and ranking failure.

  2. Make audit fit the article

    Judge the promised outcome, detect omissions and structural failures, and expose selected facts to the auditor.

  3. Simplify the prompt stack

    One authority order, one voice, form-owned structure, and short mechanical rules. Move historical examples into evaluation fixtures.

  4. Compare using frozen inputs

    Use the same saved brief and evidence for current and simplified prompts. Judge drafts blind for accuracy, brief fulfillment, readability, and editing effort—not the pipeline’s own score.
