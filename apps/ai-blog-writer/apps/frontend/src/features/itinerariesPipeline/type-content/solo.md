# Solo itinerary titles

Title guidance for itineraries built for **one traveler**: freedom to roam, easy-to-do-alone plans, safe and social where it counts.

## What the title should foreground

- That it's **for solo travelers**: "solo," "on your own," "traveling alone," "for one."
- A **reassurance or upside** when it sharpens the promise: safe, easy to do alone, good for meeting people.
- The **destination** (and neighborhood when it's the draw) so it ties to a searchable place.

## Good title moves

- Intent + place + scope: *"A Solo 3-Day Itinerary in Tokyo"*.
- Lead with the upside: *"Safe, Walkable, and Easy Alone: 2 Days Solo in Lisbon"*.
- Speak to the solo reader: *"Traveling Alone in Mexico City: A Weekend Guide"*.

## Avoid

- Generic "solo adventure" with no place, length, or angle.
- Implying loneliness; keep it confident and inviting.
