# Adventure itinerary titles

Title guidance for itineraries built around **the active outdoors**: hiking, water, heights, adrenaline, getting off pavement.

## What the title should foreground

- The **adventure angle**, plainly: "adventure," "outdoor," "active," "adrenaline," "off the beaten path."
- A **concrete activity** when it sharpens the promise: hiking, diving, climbing, rafting, surfing.
- The **destination** (and the landscape/area when it's the draw) so it ties to a searchable place.

## Good title moves

- Intent + place + scope: *"An Adventure-Packed 3 Days in Costa Rica"*.
- Name the activities: *"Hike, Surf, and Zipline: 2 Active Days in Bali"*.
- Frame the energy: *"The Adrenaline Guide to a Weekend in Queenstown"*.

## Avoid

- Generic "adventure of a lifetime" with no place or activity.
- Overpromising extreme thrills the itinerary can't back up.
