# Neighborhood guide titles

Title guidance for itineraries built around **a single neighborhood or district**: hyper-local, deep on one area, walkable.

## What the title should foreground

- The **specific neighborhood**, by name—this is the primary entity, ahead of the city.
- A **local-depth signal** when it sharpens the promise: "neighborhood guide," "a day in," "what to do in," "local's guide."
- The **city** as context after the neighborhood, so search can place it.

## Good title moves

- Neighborhood + intent + scope: *"A Day in Shimokitazawa: Tokyo's Vintage Neighborhood"*.
- Lead with local depth: *"The Local's Guide to Condesa, Mexico City"*.
- Frame the walkable promise: *"What to Do in Lisbon's Alfama in One Day"*.

## Avoid

- City-only titles; the neighborhood must lead.
- "Hidden gem neighborhood" filler with no name or angle.
