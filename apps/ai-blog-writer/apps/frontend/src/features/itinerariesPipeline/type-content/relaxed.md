# Relaxed / slow-travel itinerary titles

Title guidance for itineraries built around **unhurried days**: few stops, deep not wide, rest built in—slow travel.

## What the title should foreground

- The **relaxed pace**, plainly: "relaxed," "slow," "laid-back," "unhurried," "easy," "take it slow."
- A **calm upside** when it sharpens the promise: no rush, no checklist, breathing room.
- The **destination** (and neighborhood when it's the draw) so it ties to a searchable place.

## Good title moves

- Intent + place + scope: *"A Relaxed 3-Day Itinerary in Kyoto"*.
- Lead with the pace: *"Slow Travel in Tuscany: An Unhurried Long Weekend"*.
- Frame the mood: *"No Rush: How to Spend 2 Easy Days in Lisbon"*.

## Avoid

- Generic "relax and unwind" with no place or angle.
- Packing the title with activities; the promise is doing less, better.
