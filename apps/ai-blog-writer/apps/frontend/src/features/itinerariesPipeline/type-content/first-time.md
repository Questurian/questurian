# First-time visitor itinerary titles

Title guidance for itineraries built for **first-timers**: the essential classics, sensible orientation, nothing missed.

## What the title should foreground

- That it's **for first-timers**: "first time," "first visit," "first-timer's guide," "if it's your first time."
- A **reassurance** when it sharpens the promise: the essentials, the classics, the must-sees, nothing missed.
- The **destination** (city level, usually) so it maps to how first-timers search.

## Good title moves

- Intent + place + scope: *"A First-Timer's 3-Day Itinerary in Rome"*.
- Lead with the reassurance: *"The Essentials: 2 Days in Tokyo for First-Time Visitors"*.
- Speak to the reader directly: *"First Time in Paris? Here's a Perfect Long Weekend"*.

## Avoid

- "Hidden gems" framing; first-timers want the classics done right.
- Vague "must-see" with no place or scope.
