# Budget itinerary titles

Title guidance for itineraries built around **value**: free and cheap things to do, smart spending, no premium upsell.

## What the title should foreground

- The **budget angle**, plainly: "on a budget," "cheap," "affordable," "free things to do," "for under $X."
- A **concrete value signal** when it sharpens the promise: free, under a price point, without overspending.
- The **destination** (and neighborhood when it's the draw) so it ties to a searchable place.

## Good title moves

- Intent + place + scope: *"An Affordable 3-Day Itinerary in Bangkok"*.
- Lead with the value hook: *"Free and Cheap: 2 Days in Lisbon on a Budget"*.
- Use a price frame when natural: *"How to Do Mexico City for Under $50 a Day"*.

## Avoid

- Vague "budget-friendly" with no place or concrete saving.
- Implying it's worse, not just cheaper; keep it smart, not stingy.
