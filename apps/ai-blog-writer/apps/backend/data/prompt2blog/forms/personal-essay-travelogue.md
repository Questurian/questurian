## Use when

Use when a real journey, encounter, or personal change is the primary material and the author has supplied notes, memories, journals, photographs, recordings, or a draft. The commission should identify the experience and the insight it may illuminate without predetermining a false lesson.

## Do not use when

Do not use if no first-person material exists, the writer would need to impersonate an experience, or practical advice is the true purpose. Use Destination Guide or Service Guide for reader planning, Feature/Profile for reported narrative, or Opinion/Column for argument.

## Reader promise

Give readers an honest, specific account with a meaningful reflective arc. Sensory detail, dialogue, chronology, emotion, and uncertainty must come from supplied material rather than invention.

## Required evidence

Require attributable firsthand material for every scene, quotation, sequence, and personal claim. External sources may verify place, history, or practical context, but they cannot manufacture lived experience. Preserve uncertainty where memory is incomplete. Protect private people through approved anonymization without creating composite events or characters. Distinguish what the author observed, inferred, and later learned.

## Allowed structures

1. Opening scene, journey or encounter, turning point, reflective return.
2. Chronological travelogue with selected moments and a restrained conclusion.
3. Braided essay alternating documented experience with carefully sourced context.

Select scenes for the approved insight, not merely itinerary completeness. Practical context should support the narrative, not replace it.

## Failure modes

Avoid invented dialogue, false epiphany, tourist-as-hero framing, exoticism, compressed timelines presented literally, and universal claims drawn from one trip. Do not add danger, romance, or sensory detail for drama. Missing firsthand support requires a research gap.

## Headline note

Favor a concrete image, journey, or honest realization. First person is acceptable. Avoid claiming transformation or revelation stronger than the supplied account supports.
