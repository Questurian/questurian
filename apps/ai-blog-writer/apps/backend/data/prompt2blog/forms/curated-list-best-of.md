## Use when

Use when selection is the reader value: the best museums for families, notable neighborhood restaurants, worthwhile day trips, or a short set of places matching a defined need. The commission must bound geography, audience, category, and selection criteria.

## Do not use when

Do not use for an unranked destination inventory, a provider directory, or a comparison requiring a single winner. Use Destination Guide, Service Guide, or Comparison. Do not call a list “best” when evidence only establishes popularity or existence.

## Reader promise

Offer a defensible, useful shortlist. Explain why every item belongs, who it suits, meaningful tradeoffs, and current practical details. The list should reduce choice, not reproduce search results.

## Required evidence

Verify identity, location, current operation, relevant offerings, access, and any stated price or booking requirement. Use evidence appropriate to the selection criteria; popularity rankings cannot establish quality by themselves. Disclose whether the list is ranked, alphabetical, geographic, or thematic. Keep inclusion standards consistent across items and identify material evidence gaps.

## Allowed structures

1. Selection method, ranked entries, best-for labels, practical comparison, decision shortcut.
2. Thematic groups, concise entries, tradeoffs, map or route logic in prose.
3. Unranked editor’s picks, each tied to one explicit reader need, followed by choosing advice.

Item count should follow evidence and reader utility, never a predetermined SEO number.

## Failure modes

Avoid arbitrary rankings, repetitive descriptions, unsupported superlatives, affiliate-style product language, and entries included only to reach a number. Do not mix categories with incompatible criteria. Never invent a visit, meal, or evaluation.

## Headline note

Name category, place, and audience or criterion. Use a number only when final inventory is stable. “Best” requires stated editorial judgment; otherwise prefer “worthwhile,” “notable,” or a direct category label.
