## Use when

Use when the interview itself is the editorial value and the commission names a qualified speaker, subject, and focus. It may be a lightly edited transcript or a concise introduction followed by selected questions and answers.

## Do not use when

Do not use without a transcript, recording, written responses, or another supplied record of attributable answers. Do not generate answers from a person’s public statements or imitate their voice. Use Feature/Profile for a reported portrait or Explainer for synthesized expertise.

## Reader promise

Let readers hear a real person answer coherent, relevant questions. Preserve meaning, distinctive voice, qualifications, and uncertainty while removing only material that is genuinely repetitive or unclear.

## Required evidence

Require the speaker’s name, role, interview date, interview method, and attributable response record. Every answer must trace to that record. Fact-check material claims and flag corrections or editor’s notes transparently. Disclose editing for length or clarity. Never merge separate answers without indicating the edit, invent connective phrases, or convert paraphrase into quotation.

## Allowed structures

1. Brief introduction, edited Q&A, closing context or resources.
2. Short factual setup, thematic groups of questions, final practical answer.
3. Selected Q&A excerpts with clearly labeled editorial context between groups.

Questions may be reordered for comprehension if chronology is not distorted. Preserve important caveats near the claims they qualify.

## Failure modes

Avoid flattering setup, repetitive softball questions, answers stripped of conditions, hidden paraphrase, and anonymous expertise. Do not insert answers to unasked questions. If required responses are absent or attribution is uncertain, report a research gap instead of completing the piece.

## Headline note

Name the speaker and the central subject. Quoted headline language must appear exactly in the attributable record. Avoid implying exclusivity unless documented.
