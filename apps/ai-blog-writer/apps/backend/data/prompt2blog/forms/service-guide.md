## Use when

Use for a bounded service question such as choosing an airport transfer, using a rail pass, finding accessible lodging, arranging connectivity, or navigating a booking rule. The commission should name the reader, decision, location, and practical outcome.

## Do not use when

Do not use for an overview of an entire destination, a strict sequence of actions, or a ranked product list. Use Destination Guide, How-To/Checklist, or Curated List/Best Of. Use Explainer when understanding the system matters more than making a choice.

## Reader promise

Give the reader enough verified information to choose, book, use, or avoid a service confidently. State tradeoffs, eligibility, timing, costs, and failure points in terms relevant to the primary audience.

## Required evidence

Use official operator or authority sources for routes, rules, schedules, eligibility, and published prices. Add independent reporting or firsthand specialist evidence for reliability, usability, and real-world friction. Capture currency, tax or fee inclusion, effective dates, booking windows, cancellation rules, and accessibility limits when relevant. Never infer availability from an outdated listing.

## Allowed structures

1. Quick recommendation, option breakdown, decision factors, booking or use instructions, cautions.
2. Reader scenario, available services, tradeoff table in prose, best fit by need, fallback plan.
3. Before arrival, at point of use, troubleshooting, alternatives.

Organize around the approved decision, not around providers’ marketing categories.

## Failure modes

Avoid generic tips, undisclosed sponsored framing, price claims without dates, absolute reliability claims, and recommendations with no audience fit. Do not silently treat missing operational detail as unchanged. If a critical rule or availability claim cannot be verified, expose the gap.

## Headline note

Name the task or decision, location, and meaningful constraint. Prefer specific utility over “best way” unless evidence supports one best choice for the stated audience.
