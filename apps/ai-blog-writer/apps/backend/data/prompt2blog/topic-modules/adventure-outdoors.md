## Research questions

What route, activity, environment, duration, skill, fitness, guide, permit, equipment, and emergency plan are required? Which seasonal, altitude, wildlife, water, fire, or weather conditions affect feasibility? What lower-risk alternatives fit the audience?

## Preferred sources

Use park and land authorities, route managers, rescue and weather agencies, licensed operators, recognized outdoor bodies, current maps, and attributable firsthand reports. Commercial descriptions cannot establish safety or route status alone.

## Freshness

Verify permits, access, trail or water conditions, closures, operator status, and warnings close to publication. Clearly date condition-dependent evidence.

## Factual limits and gaps

Do not declare an activity easy, safe, family-friendly, or suitable for beginners without defined conditions. Never invent route detail or substitute generic preparation advice for local evidence. Warn when access, rescue coverage, required experience, guide rules, or a load-bearing condition report is missing.
