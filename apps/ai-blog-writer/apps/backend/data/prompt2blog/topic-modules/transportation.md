## Research questions

Which journey or network must readers navigate? What routes, transfers, operating windows, fares, payment methods, booking rules, luggage limits, and accessibility conditions matter? How do options differ in time, cost, reliability, comfort, and risk for the primary audience?

## Preferred sources

Prefer transport authorities, operators, airports, official maps and timetables, accessibility documentation, and current service notices. Use reputable local reporting or attributable firsthand evidence for recurring friction and real-world usability.

## Freshness

Treat schedules, fares, routes, construction, strikes, payment systems, and entry procedures as volatile. Record effective or retrieval dates and account for day-of-week or seasonal differences.

## Factual limits and gaps

Do not convert scheduled time into guaranteed journey time or one trip into a reliability rate. Separate official rules from observed enforcement. Warn when a critical connection, fare, accessibility feature, safety condition, or current operating status cannot be verified.
