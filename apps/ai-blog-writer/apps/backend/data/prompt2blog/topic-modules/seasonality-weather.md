## Research questions

Which months, elevations, microclimates, events, or activities matter? What are typical ranges, rainfall patterns, daylight, extreme-weather risks, crowd cycles, closures, and price effects? How much year-to-year variability should readers expect?

## Preferred sources

Use national meteorological agencies, climate normals, park or destination authorities, disaster agencies, event organizers, operators, and reputable local reporting. Forecasts support imminent conditions, not general seasonal claims.

## Freshness

Use an appropriate climate-normal period and identify it. Recheck current seasonal closures, event dates, active warnings, and anomalous conditions close to publication.

## Factual limits and gaps

Do not promise weather, call one month universally best, or confuse climate averages with forecasts. Distinguish coast, valley, mountain, and neighborhood conditions where meaningful. Warn when activity access depends on unverified conditions, event dates are unsettled, or cited averages do not fit the approved geography.
