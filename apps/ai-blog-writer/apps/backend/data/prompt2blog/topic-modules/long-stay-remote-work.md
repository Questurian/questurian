## Research questions

Who may stay, for how long, and under what work conditions? What housing, connectivity, workspace, banking, tax, insurance, healthcare, time-zone, community, and daily-life constraints matter? Which claims apply to visitors, residents, employees, contractors, or dependants?

## Preferred sources

Use immigration and tax authorities, official labor guidance, telecom data, housing rules, healthcare sources, reputable market research, and attributable long-stay experience. Forums may reveal research questions but cannot settle legal or typical-cost claims.

## Freshness

Recheck visas, remote-work permissions, tax thresholds, rental rules, connectivity products, and prices. Date market and speed evidence.

## Factual limits and gaps

Do not give individualized legal or tax advice, equate visa permission with work authorization, or infer citywide livability from one neighborhood. Separate published internet speed from usable service. Warn when work legality, tax exposure, housing assumptions, healthcare access, or current connectivity evidence remains unresolved.
