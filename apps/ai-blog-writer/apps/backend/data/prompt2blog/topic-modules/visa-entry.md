## Research questions

Which passport, residence status, travel purpose, destination, transit point, and intended stay apply? Is a visa, authorization, insurance, onward ticket, registration, fee, or minimum passport validity required? Which authority decides, and where can the traveler verify before departure?

## Preferred sources

Use destination immigration authorities, embassies or consulates, foreign ministries, official application portals, and carrier documentation for boarding requirements. Specialist reporting may explain implementation but cannot override official rules.

## Freshness

Entry rules are highly volatile. Verify immediately before publication, preserve effective and retrieval dates, and link readers to the deciding authority.

## Factual limits and gaps

Never generalize one nationality’s rules to another, promise admission, or present anecdotal enforcement as policy. Distinguish visa exemption, visa on arrival, electronic authorization, and permission to work. Warn when nationality-specific eligibility, transit rules, fees, or the official source cannot be confirmed.
