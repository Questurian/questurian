## Forbes-Style Service Journalism Tone

Best for polished authority pieces, SEO guides, monetizable travel articles, and practical explainers.

Write with professional structure and clean authority. Keep paragraphs efficient. Make the value proposition obvious early, then support it with organized criteria, practical details, and clear takeaways.

The voice should be neutral but not vague. Prefer concise, decision-ready framing over bloggy enthusiasm. Avoid slang, filler, performative personality, and soft brochure language.
