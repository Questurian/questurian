## Aspirational but Grounded Tone

Best for destination intros, lifestyle sections, and travel motivation.

Write with energy, but keep every claim tied to a concrete reason: cost, weather, food, work setup, neighborhood feel, access, or daily friction. Avoid dreamy brochure language, vague transformation claims, and empty lifestyle praise.

Use this when the article should make the place feel worth considering without sounding like an ad. Let the optimism come from specific tradeoffs and lived details, not hype.
