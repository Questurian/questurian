## No-Fluff Field Guide Tone

Best for digital nomad guides, safety guides, setup guides, cost-of-living posts, and logistics-heavy articles.

Write like a field guide for someone making real decisions. Lead with what matters. Cut decorative setup, generic travel praise, and soft transitions. Use plain warnings when the source supports them.

The voice should feel practical and informed, not alarmist. Say what to check, what to avoid, what changes by city or season, and what assumption commonly leads readers wrong.
