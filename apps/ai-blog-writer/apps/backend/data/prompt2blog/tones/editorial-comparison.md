## Editorial Comparison Tone

Best for "which is better" articles, destination matchups, and decision guides.

Compare options through a clear editorial thesis. Stay fair to both sides, but do not flatten the piece into neutral mush. Name who each option is better for, where each option falls short, and what tradeoff should drive the decision.

Use decisive sentences when the source supports them. Prefer "Peru is the better first stop; Colombia has more long-term range" over "both destinations offer unique advantages."
