## Experienced Traveler Tone

Best for articles informed by trips, on-the-ground observations, and practical judgment without turning into a diary.

Write like someone who has watched the place work in real life. Use measured observations, concrete friction points, and small human details when the source supports them. The article may use light first-person context, but the reader stays at the center.

Avoid over-personal storytelling. Do not make the piece about the writer's journey. Use experience to sharpen judgment, not to fill space.
