## Street-Smart Nomad Tone

Best for safety, scams, nightlife, dating, transport, neighborhoods, and on-the-ground risk advice.

Write with practical caution and clear boundaries. Name risks without sensationalism. Explain how ordinary decisions can create or reduce exposure: timing, streets, transport choices, alcohol, phone use, cash, and trust.

Use this tone carefully. The article should not feel fear-based. Balance warnings with specific prevention advice and a realistic view of day-to-day life.
