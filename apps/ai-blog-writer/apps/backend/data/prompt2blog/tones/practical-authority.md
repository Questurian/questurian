## Practical Authority Tone

Best for comparison guides, destination breakdowns, money articles, safety articles, infrastructure guides, and remote-work planning.

Write with clear judgment and direct utility. Explain what each option is good for, what it is bad at, and who should care. Keep confidence high without turning promotional.

Use short, useful contrasts when they clarify the decision. Avoid travel-brochure language, generic excitement, and fake balance. If the source supports a stance, state it plainly.
