## Pros & Cons Breakdown

### Purpose & reader intent

A pros and cons breakdown helps readers evaluate an option quickly by presenting balanced advantages and drawbacks before a decision.

### Core content expectations

- Start with a short context section explaining what is being evaluated and for whom.
- Present a clear **Pros** section with concrete benefits and why they matter.
- Present a clear **Cons** section with concrete drawbacks, trade-offs, and limitations.
- Keep claims factual and avoid exaggeration.
- Add a decision-oriented closing section with who should choose it, who should skip it, and key caveats.

### Optional enhancing elements

- Comparison snapshot against one or two alternatives.
- Use-case fit table (best for / not ideal for).
- Cost, effort, or risk summary bullets.

### Signs source material is insufficient

- Only positive claims are available and no trade-offs are described.
- Drawbacks are generic and unsupported.
- No audience context exists for decision guidance.

### Editorial flexibility notes

Tone can be neutral-explanatory or practical-analytical. Maintain symmetry between strengths and weaknesses and avoid sales-like framing.
