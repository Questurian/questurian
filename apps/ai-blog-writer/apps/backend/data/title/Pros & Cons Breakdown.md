## Pros & Cons Breakdown

- **Purpose of titles:** Signal a balanced evaluation and clear decision value.
- **Title structure rules:**
  - Name the subject first.
  - Include “Pros and Cons” or “Advantages and Drawbacks”.
  - Add audience or use-case context when relevant.
- **Style rules:**
  - Keep titles specific and factual.
  - Avoid clickbait and absolute claims.
  - Keep title concise and scannable.

### Quality checklist

- Does the title clearly indicate balanced evaluation?
- Is the evaluated subject explicit?
- Does wording avoid hype and overpromising?
