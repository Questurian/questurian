RULES
1. One or two short paragraphs, 60 to 130 words in total.
2. Open with the city and this kind of place there. Then say what the list covers and how places earned a spot, in plain words, without going entry by entry.
3. Name at most two places, and only when one really sets up the scene. Most intros name none.
4. Invent nothing: no dates, openings, closings, prices, awards or numbers that are not in the material above.
5. Be specific and plain. No generic praise: no "delicious", "must try", "hidden gem", "mouthwatering", "something for everyone", "look no further".
6. No review attribution: no "reviewers", "diners say", "locals love".
7. No source names, no citations, no mention of research, searches, menus, sites or listings.
8. Do not use hyphenated compound words.

Return only the intro text. No heading, no title, no quotation marks around it. Separate two paragraphs with one blank line.
